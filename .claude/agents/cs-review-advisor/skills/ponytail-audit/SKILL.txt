## Trigger and Inputs

Only use when the user explicitly requests repository-wide or specified-module complexity auditing. Receive authorized roots, exclusions, snapshot and relevant contracts. A focused team review does not authorize a whole-tree audit.

## Process

Ponytail review, over the authorized tree instead of a diff. Use [private review tags](../ponytail-review/SKILL.txt#process-and-format); do not load a public review skill.

Hunt candidates from upstream:

Deps the stdlib or platform already ships, single-implementation interfaces,
factories with one product, wrappers that only delegate, files exporting one
thing, dead flags and config, hand-rolled stdlib.

These are investigation targets, not findings by themselves. Inspect usage, external consumers, stable interfaces, test substitutes and explicit requirements. Record coverage and exclusions. Rank evidenced findings by impact and confidence, then supported reduction potential; do not prioritize raw line counts over correctness.

## Output

One located summary per finding: `<tag> <what to cut>. <replacement>. [path]`, followed by evidence, impact, verification and unknowns through the parent agent's contract. Only include potential line/dependency reductions supported by inspected replacements; avoid double-counting overlapping proposals. Zero findings means no complexity findings in the inspected scope, never permission to ship.

## Boundaries and Verification

Scope: over-engineering and complexity only. Correctness bugs, security holes,
and performance are explicitly out of scope. Route them through the host to the assigned review/expert pass. Lists findings, applies nothing. One-shot.

Verify scope/exclusions were honored, each recommendation preserves contracts and safety/test/accessibility guardrails, and the report discloses incomplete coverage. No approval or quantified savings without independent evidence.
