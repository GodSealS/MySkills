# Blender Camera

For `cs-blender-operator` only. Follow the [parent contract](../../../cs-blender-operator.md).
Load for camera creation/placement, composition, lens/projection and depth of field.

## Work

1. Identify the intended scene/camera, active scene camera, camera data users, parent/constraints,
   frame, output aspect ratio and target bounds. Distinguish moving the viewport for inspection
   from changing a render camera. Isolate shared camera data for a local lens edit when needed.
2. Position and aim the camera using the target's world-space bounds and actual transforms.
   Blender cameras look along local -Z with local Y up; account for parents and constraints
   when converting the desired world orientation to the stored transform.
3. Select the requested projection and framing. Perspective framing depends on focal length,
   sensor fit and aspect ratio; orthographic framing uses orthographic scale. Adjust near/far
   clipping, lens shift and focus deliberately, and keep resolution changes within scope.
4. Assign the active scene camera when the requested result must affect rendering. A moved
   camera that is not active may leave the final image unchanged. Configure focus target or
   distance and aperture only if depth of field is requested or part of the accepted look.
5. Load [Animation](../blender-animation/SKILL.txt) for camera or lens animation. For a static
   composition, preserve existing keyframes/constraints and report conflicts before replacing them.

## Verification

- Inspect through the intended camera at the final aspect ratio, checking framing, clipping,
  horizon/orientation and subject visibility. A free-viewport screenshot is insufficient.
- Check the actual scene camera and its world transform, projection/lens and focus values.
- For animated shots, sample intermediate frames as well as endpoints for collisions,
  unwanted occlusion and focus drift. Restore temporary preview settings after inspection.

## Lookup

Use the installed-version [camera manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cameras.html)
and [Camera API](https://docs.blender.org/api/current/bpy.types.Camera.html).
